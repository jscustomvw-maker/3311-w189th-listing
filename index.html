Completely Remodeled. Move-In Ready.

Welcome to 3311 W 189th St, a beautifully reimagined two bedroom, two bath home in the heart of North Torrance. This 864 square foot residence has been completely remodeled from top to bottom, offering the rare combination of a move in ready interior and a location close to shopping, dining, and easy freeway access throughout the South Bay.

Step inside to find a fresh, light filled interior anchored by a fully remodeled kitchen featuring brand new stainless steel appliances, ready for everyday cooking and entertaining alike. New waterproof flooring flows throughout the home, paired with new windows that bring in natural light while adding energy efficiency. Both bathrooms have been fully renovated with clean, modern finishes, giving this home a polished feel from the moment you walk through the door.

Beyond the cosmetics, this home has been upgraded where it truly counts. A new driveway leads to a convenient one car drive through garage, adding flexibility for parking or storage.
